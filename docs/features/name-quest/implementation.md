# NAME QUEST: implementation

**Updated:** 2026-11-21 · [Design](design.md) · [Testing](testing.md)

## Approach
NAME QUEST lives in its own `source/quest*` files with no SDL or PS3 headers, so `make test` builds them on the Mac. `main.cpp` owns one `Quest`, opens it from the main menu, steps it at 60 Hz with player 1's `Actions`, plays its requested sounds, prints its log lines and draws it. The quest is one mode machine (`QuestMode`: slots, delete, class, intro, play, dead) in `questUpdate`, with one `switch`.

Movement is `moveHero`: grace timers, ladders, walking, jumping (buffered, coyote time, drop-through, the Thief's double jump), the jump cut, gravity, currents and vents, then an x move and a y move each resolved against the tiles. The reachability test runs the same function.

## How main.cpp runs the quest
- The main menu's NAME QUEST item stops the music, calls `questOpen` with the EBOOT's folder and sets `quest.devAids`.
- `questUpdate` runs only in frames that started in `SCENE_QUEST`, so the press that opened the quest or resumed it from pause never also acts in it. Presses read in a frame that ran no game step carry over to the next frame.
- After every step the press fields of player 1's actions are cleared, so a press acts once.
- Start (or a lost controller) in play opens main.cpp's pause menu with `questPaused` set; the pause and settings screens draw `questDraw` behind them and never start a song. RETURN TO MENU calls `questSave`.
- Quitting the app (the PS button menu's Quit Game, `SDL_QUIT`) saves the slot when NAME QUEST is in play or paused over it (`questCanPause`), after the main loop (spec §17).
- `quest.logLines` are printed with `debugNetPrintf` every frame and cleared; `quest.wantsMainMenu` returns to the main menu with the NAME QUEST item selected.
- The 5-second fps line shows `quest <world>.<area>` instead of the wave and score.

## Data and config
- **Config constants:** movement numbers at the top of `quest.cpp` (spec §5); class stats and walk speeds in `CLASSES` (`quest_stats.cpp`); combat numbers at the top of `quest_combat.cpp` (spec §5-§9); death, banner and camera timings in `quest.cpp`.
- **Persistence:** `<EBOOT folder>/quest1.txt` .. `quest3.txt`, key=value lines (spec §17).
- **Dev aids:** `NAMEGAME_QUEST='slot=3 class=0 world=8 area=0'` starts straight into play; L2+L3 test mode; Select warps to the next area.

## Combat (phase 2)
- `questUpdate` in play calls `updateEffects` (particles, numbers, skill shapes run even during hit-stop), then `updateCombat`, which handles timers, dodge, attacks, then calls `moveHero` with the inputs adjusted (knockback, roll, dash, root, x0.5 walk), then enemies, shots and damage. `die` drops the echo; the echo is touched in `questUpdate`.
- `enterArea` calls `spawnEnemies` (letters A B D E F whose bit in `Quest::killed[world][area]` is clear); death clears every killed list.
- Enemies are drawn from the shooter's 1x sprites doubled at start-up into chunky 2x copies (like the old-school heroes); new enemies use `PLACEHOLDER_ENEMY` until their world's phase. Hitbox: sprite minus 2 dp each side.
- `Hero::swing` ids make each melee swing hit an enemy once; shots keep a bitmask of the enemies they hit.

## RPG systems (phase 3)
- Play mode collects what the hero touches (N, T, C, M, P) in one pass over the area's letters, lights unlit braziers on touch, then handles Up in the spec's order. Items and the drink timer run in `questUpdate` before `updateCombat`; the drink's x0.4 and the feather's x1.15 scale the stick in `updateCombat`.
- `QMODE_DIALOGUE`, `QMODE_MENU` (one `MenuState`, one `switch (kind)` on X), `QMODE_SHOP` and `QMODE_MAP` each have one case in `questUpdate`; they draw the play picture behind their panel (the map is full-screen). `DialogueState::after` opens the next mode when the last page closes.
- `rest` clears every killed list, calls `resetHazards` (reloads the area's tiles: crumbles in phase 4, icicles in phase 5), respawns enemies, heals, sets the respawn point and saves. Travel sets the checkpoint, `enterAtCheckpoint`, then `rest`.
- `objectIndex(world, area, tile, letter)` numbers checkpoints and surveyors across a world (shared by the rules, the HUD map and the drawing). `openGate` and `openShortcut` are public: mini-bosses call `openGate` from phase 4; `touchLever` in `quest_combat.cpp` calls `openShortcut` from every hero hit box, circle and shot.
- `Quest::ambient` is set on area entry; main.cpp calls `audioSetQuestSound(place, quest.arenaLocked, music not OFF)` every frame; it returns early while nothing changed. It stops only a quest song, never the shooter's.

## Bosses and world 1 (phase 4)
- `enterArea` clears `bosses`, `markers` and `arenaLocked`, spawns enemies, logs the area, then calls `startArenaFight`, which spawns the world's mini-boss at `Q` (unless bit 63 of its area's killed list is set) or big boss at `W` (unless `SaveData::bosses` has the world). `die` ends the fight.
- `questUpdate` calls `updateBosses` after `updateCombat` unless a hit-stop froze the step. While `arenaLocked`, the hero is clamped inside the side edges and the top, edge links and portals do nothing, and the bottom is a pit.
- Hero attacks reach bosses through the same paths as enemies: `hitBox`, `hitCircle`, shots (`Projectile::bossMask`) and Chain Lightning call `hitBoss` (no knockback, no stun); `bossHittable` excludes the phase shift and the Star Mage's fade.
- Telegraphs are `Quest::markers` (dashed outlines); the beam and the growing ball are `FX_BEAM` and `FX_BALL` effects. `bossPose`, `bossVisibility` and `electricFloor` tell `quest_draw.cpp` what to show.
- World 1's art is generated by `docs/work-plan/name-quest/art-tools/world1.py` (via `make_quest_art.py`); characters are art pixels with a baked outline, doubled, like the heroes.
- `make quest-maps` builds `quest_tests` and runs it with `--maps`: two screenshots per area of every world into `build/maps`, converted to `shots/maps`.

## Deviations from design
- `Hero` has two fields the plan didn't list: `dropTimer` (down + X drop-through) and `jumped` (for the jump sound).
- `saveWrite` takes an optional `renameFailed` flag so `questSave` can log `rename failed, wrote directly`.
- Holding Select 2 s goes to the TEST GROUNDS area 0 (`Actions::selectHeld`, phase 2).
- The user's request (not in the spec): the Thief's basic stab with up held (R1 + up) aims up: its 16 x 12 box turns upright (12 x 16) above the head, with an upright arc (`Hero::attackUp`; test `thiefStabsUp`). The body keeps the sideways stab frames.
- Vents give a net lift of 900 px/s² (gravity is cancelled, then 900 more), up to a rise of 160 px/s.
- Crumbling blocks stay solid until phase 4; spikes, electric panels and fire jets hurt since phase 2.
- Phase 2: `Enemy` has `world` (TEST GROUNDS area 3 borrows world 2's charger and dropper so all 7 behaviours appear; world 1's roster has 5), `w`, `h`, `names`, `onGround`, `stunTimer`, the swoop target, swing ids and the wraith's stolen names. `Projectile` has `mult`, `strong` and `hitMask`; `DamageNumber` has `life` and `names`; `Effect` holds skill shapes for drawing.
- The puffer stays deflated 1 s after inflating before it can inflate again (otherwise a melee hero within 32 px could never hit it).
- Skill timings the spec leaves open: Power Slash's box is live 0.1-0.25 s of its 0.6 s; Rising Slash and Lion's Roar 0-0.15 s; Storm Volley fires at 0, 0.225, 0.45 s; Frost Orb's range is 200 px; Arrow Rain arrows pass through tiles for their first 120 px of fall.
- `levelCost(39)` is 102,067 by the spec's formula; the spec's example says 102,064. Its 29→30 example (10,646) is off too: the formula gives 16,484. Code follows the formula; the examples are reported, not changed.
- A strong attack cancels a basic attack in progress (the spec is silent): otherwise both used one swing id and the basic box used up the skill's hit.
- `drawSpriteSolid` takes a `flip` (enemy flashes and the Shadow Partner face their way).
- Namula's background has no hearth glow on the near walls: a glowing edge read as a platform.
- Phase 3: `AreaDef::npcs` holds 8 (Namula's cliffs have 7 NPCs; the spec's `npcs[2]` can't hold them). Unused entries must be written as -1 (0 is Ilsa); the area test checks it.
- Phase 3: `SAVED_WORLDS` is 9, so TEST GROUNDS' visited areas, chests and lever are saved too (`visited8=` and so on, beside the spec's 0-7).
- Phase 3: `AFTER_JOB` opens `MENU_JOB_CONFIRM` (`BECOME <JOB>?` YES / NOT YET); YES grants the tier.
- Phase 3: Circle closes a dialogue without opening its `after`.
- Phase 3: world 6's chest 5 is WHETSTONE + IRON TONIC in the spec, but one `ChestContent` holds one item; it gives the WHETSTONE only until phase 8 decides.
- Phase 3: Namula's cliffs: one N moved onto the well's rock (Sela by the well). Shore: the Stranger left, Claude by the portals, Tobin and Garrick by the hut. Garrick, the Stranger, Calder and Claude have no sprite yet (their phases).
- Phase 3: World 1's entry was not lit from the start (world 1 had no areas). Phase 4 lights it: `SaveData::lit` starts at bits 0 and 2, and loading a slot sets both.
- Phase 3: `drawMenu` widens its panel for a title that doesn't fit (long checkpoint names); the shooter's titles fit, so its menus are unchanged.
- Phase 3: the HUD item count reads `X3`: the 3x5 font has no lowercase `x`.

- Phase 4: the plan's `Boss` struct is `QuestBoss` (the shooter's `game.h` already has a `Boss`). It adds `names`, `big` and the hero's swing ids. `startArenaFight` is a public function (the plan puts the arena check in area entry) so tests can start fights in hand-made arenas.
- Phase 4: `EnemyType::frames` holds 4 movement frames plus `tell` and `attack` poses (spec §22); worlds 2-6 still use 2 doubled shooter frames. The jumper's tell is its last 0.2 s on the ground; the shooter's attack pose shows 0.2 s after a shot.
- Phase 4: electric panels hurt only while the hero overlaps the panel tile (phase 2 also hurt one tile above it). Panels are not solid, so they sit in the air row on a floor; the old rule made a hero two tiles above a lit panel take damage.
- Phase 4: numbers the spec leaves open: boss shots 4 x 4 at 100 dp/s; the Overseer's FAN 5 shots 20 degrees apart, straight down in the middle; its slam lands bigAtk while dropping, it rises at 100 dp/s; every attack recovers 0.6 s; the Star Mage teleports before every attack, its beam runs from the wand's star down to the arena floor (one-way platforms don't stop it), and the second beam goes 64 dp toward the hero (the other side if that leaves the arena); summons appear beside the Overseer and a quarter of the arena in from each side for the Star Mage. The electric floor's damage is the boss's attack (0.25).
- Phase 4: basic arrows fly 6-8 dp below the hero's top, about 7-9 dp above the floor, so a ground enemy whose hitbox is under ~8 dp tall can't be hit by them. The arrow's size and the hitbox rule are spec numbers, so enemies are drawn at least 12 dp tall with their outline (§22's minimum); the CRAWLER was 11 dp and was redrawn up on its legs (10 dp hitbox). Worlds 2-6's placeholder sprites (6-8 dp hitboxes) must meet this when their phases redraw them; `arrowsHitEveryGroundEnemy` checks world 1.
- Phase 4: `WorldArt::pit` draws a pit's bottom 2 rows as open space (world 1), so a pit never reads as floor.
- Phase 4: TEST GROUNDS has two boss arenas (areas 4 and 5, reached by a portal in area 3) with world 1's bosses, for Mac checks and tests before world 1's areas exist. Killing its Overseer opens the TEST GROUNDS gate.
- Phase 4: "the Overseer hovers 50 px above the floor" is read as its centre 50 dp above the floor. Measured to its bottom (the first build), no hero could reach it: a jump's head tops out at 50 dp, and horizontal shots fly 7-9 dp up, so only its slams could be hit. The user should confirm this reading.
- Phase 4: a locked arena's linked bottom (the hangar's ladder down) holds the hero like a wall; without a link the bottom stays a pit.
- Phase 4: world 1's hatch from the docking ring down to the hangar is two `k` tiles over a ladder; area 0 and the hangar share shortcut 0, so the hangar's lever opens both ends. The hangar's arrival is one tile down its ladder so it stays free of the closed doors.
- Ambience (spec §16): Namula's 70 Hz hum and world 1's 55 Hz drone were removed. They were square waves; TV speakers drop the low tone and keep the harsh harmonics, which the user heard on the PS3 as white noise (2026-11-21, confirmed by an A/B build with Namula's hum muted). Namula keeps its crackle, world 1 its clicks. `synthesize` takes a minimum length so every loop is exactly 4 s (world 2's chirps used to end at the last chirp).
- Phase 4: the attack clock starts on the frame the attack is chosen (0 there), so a 0.8 s mark lasts exactly 48 frames.
